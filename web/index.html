CRISPR Base Editor Window

Inspect fixed CBE/ABE activity-window profiles, the intended editable base, and same-locus bystander bases directly in your browser.

Research-use heuristic. The encoded activity profiles are fixed relative weights, not experimentally calibrated editing probabilities. The tool does not model genome-wide off-target activity, sequence-context effects, cell type, chromatin state, PAM compatibility, or editor-specific experimental conditions.
